Alberta hospital dataset: source and maintenance notes

Snapshot date: 2026-10-12 (America/Edmonton).

Scope
The dataset includes every Alberta location listed under the AHS Find Healthcare
"Hospitals" service (ID 1001416), plus eight explicitly reviewed inpatient
specialist facilities missing from that service list. The service list includes
some health centres; its presence does not imply a currently staffed inpatient
bed or 24-hour emergency department. This is a directory snapshot, not a live
operating-status register or a guarantee of every legally registered hospital.

Specialist supplement:
- Alberta Hospital Edmonton
- Centennial Centre for Mental Health and Brain Injury
- Claresholm Centre for Mental Health & Addictions
- Southern Alberta Forensic Psychiatry Centre
- Villa Caritas
- Lois Hole Hospital for Women
- Mazankowski Alberta Heart Institute
- Cross Cancer Institute

The first five are identified in Alberta's designated mental health facility
list and AHS's standalone psychiatric facility definition. Lois Hole and
Mazankowski appear on the AHS hospital/facility websites list and their facility
pages list inpatient services. Cross Cancer is a tertiary cancer centre with
inpatient services, also present on the AHS Alberta Hospitals map (2022 layer).
Separately named facilities sharing a campus/address remain separate rows.

The service directory also lists Lloydminster Hospital (Saskatchewan). It is
excluded because its physical address is outside Alberta. Standalone outpatient
clinics, urgent-care-only centres absent from the Hospitals service, continuing
care/auxiliary facilities, and historical hospitals are outside this scope.

Method
Read service locations and fetch each linked service page. Take the unique AHS
facility ID from its facility link, and the name, street, municipality, province,
postal code and map latitude/longitude from labelled page fields. Read the eight
supplements from their facility pages, deduplicating by facility ID. Exclude the
Saskatchewan record. Decode HTML entities and normalize whitespace; assemble
address as "street_address, city, province postal_code". Sort by hospital name.
Keep the actual fetched URL and the inclusion-source URL in every row.

Coordinates are the decimal-degree latitude/longitude supplied to the AHS web
map, used as WGS84-compatible web-map positions. AHS does not state survey
accuracy or a datum on these pages. No geocoder, postcode centroid, or routing
service is used. They identify map locations, not validated vehicle entrances.
No emergency/operating status is inferred from inclusion.

24-hour emergency department flag (reviewed 2026-10-12)
emergency_24h is an integer: 1 when the facility's own general/pediatric
Emergency Services department has a published regular 24-hour, seven-day
schedule; 0 when there is no ED or its scheduled hours are limited. There are
94 ones and 18 zeros: 11 facilities without an ED and 7 with limited ED hours.
This follows the user's requested regular-schedule definition. Individual
temporary closures do not change the flag. Ongoing reduced weekly schedules
published for the department count as limited hours, even if temporary staffing
arrangements are the reason. This is not a live service-availability indicator.

Every facility's AHS facility page and Emergency Services link (service ID 6915,
where offered) were reviewed. Positive flags require the ED service page's
Hours of Operation to state 24-hour service; general hospital opening hours
are insufficient. Psychiatric consultation, maternity triage, urgent-care
services and a different facility's ED on the same campus do not qualify.
No-ED assessments use explicit no-ED descriptions or the reviewed specialist
facility's service inventory. Missing or ambiguous evidence is not a default 0.

Limited-hours facilities are Boyle, Consort, Grimshaw/Berwyn, Hardisty, Lamont,
Swan Hills and Two Hills. The ED schedule takes precedence over the general
facility description (notably Swan Hills). Two Hills' ED page still refers to
January 2026, so the September 15, 2026 official advisory was also reviewed:
https://www.albertahealthservices.ca/news/Page19947.aspx
It confirms an ongoing reduced weekly schedule and virtual overnight support,
not a published regular 24/7 ED schedule. General 24-hour opening at Alberta
Hospital Edmonton, Claresholm's psychiatric centre and the forensic centre
does not imply an ED. Coronation remains 1 despite individual temporary
service advisories, in accordance with the regular-schedule definition.

The separately reviewed evidence is installed in
inst/extdata/alberta-hospitals-emergency.csv. Each row has the facility ID,
name, flag, assessment, source URL, checked_on date and explanatory notes.
Source URLs identify the ED service page or the facility's no-ED inventory;
the newer advisory is used for Two Hills. The main dataset's retrieved_on
refers to the location fields; checked_on records the separate ED review.

Refresh
1. Review the scope and eight specialist additions against the official sources.
   Review ED schedules separately against each emergency evidence source and
   update alberta-hospitals-emergency.csv (including checked_on and notes).
   The location updater joins the reviewed ED flags by exact facility ID/name;
   it does not automatically refresh ED hours. Changed hospital membership or
   missing evidence stops the updater rather than assigning an unverified 0.
   When editing only ED evidence, copy its flag values by facility ID into
   alberta-hospitals.csv before rebuilding; the R builder verifies consistency.
2. From the package root, run:
   python3 data-raw/update-alberta-hospitals.py
   Rscript data-raw/create-alberta-hospitals.R
3. Review changes to inst/extdata/alberta-hospitals.csv, especially removals,
   relocations, changed facility IDs and ED schedule changes. Update counts/dates in
   R/data.R, man/alberta_hospitals.Rd, README.md, these notes and the dataset test.
4. Install/check the package and run tests/alberta-hospitals.R.

The updater needs Python >= 3.9 and curl; installed-package users need neither.
It fails on missing fields, invalid coordinates, name changes or duplicates,
and replaces the CSV only after all downloads and validations succeed.
The R build is deterministic and offline; the original CSV is bundled to make
the binary data file auditable and rebuildable without revisiting the website.
